The Heuristic Behind the Score

This calculator is a playful scoring model, not a measured probability of failure. It combines five subjective inputs to make tradeoffs visible and give you a repeatable way to compare scenarios.

The Formula Explained

The formula raises the score as Urgency, Complexity, and Importance increase, and lowers it as Skill increases. Frequency uses a deliberately exaggerated curve to make repeated exposure feel progressively more ominous.

The fixed Aggravation Factor keeps the result in a useful display range. It is a design constant, not an empirical measurement.

Why Does Murphy's Law Feel So Real?

Failures are memorable, especially when timing and stakes are bad. Use the result as a prompt to discuss assumptions and mitigations—not as evidence that a failure will occur.

Important: this tool is for entertainment and lightweight planning. Do not use it for engineering, safety, financial, medical, or operational risk certification.

Understanding Your Score

  • Scale: Scores are shown as 0% (barely cursed) to 100% (catastrophic), from the same 0.12–8.6 model.
  • Pressure builds: Higher Urgency, Complexity, and Importance push the score skyward.
  • Skill saves: Invest in Skill to tame the chaos.
  • Beware repetition: Frequent tasks (Frequency) tempt fate thanks to the sinuous 1/(1 - sin(F/10)) term.

How to cheat Murphy

  • Cut Urgency by asking: does everything really need to ship today?
  • Break huge projects into bite size work to cut Complexity.
  • Importance is a matter of perspective - is this task truly critical?
  • Build your Skill to reduce errors.
  • Batch low stakes tasks to shrink Frequency.

Use the calculator to compare scenarios, then validate real risks with appropriate domain methods.
