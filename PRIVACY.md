# Privacy

Last updated: 2026-10-10

## Data we collect

The service may receive the name, email address, and content supplied with a law submission or other message. Submissions may be anonymous.

Servers receive technical information needed to operate and protect the service, including IP address, browser and device information, visited pages, and time on page. First-party storage remembers preferences such as the selected theme.

## How data is used

- Operate, secure, and improve the archive.
- Attribute and display community contributions.
- Respond to messages and requested updates.
- Measure site and feature use.

## Third parties

- Pendo, through Novus by Pendo, processes anonymous product analytics and in-app guidance data, including a persistent anonymous visitor identifier, technical information, visited pages and URLs, and interaction events.
- Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to serve and measure advertising.
- When enabled, email delivery providers may process information needed to send requested messages or notifications.

Pendo and its providers may process data in the United States and other regions. Google describes its partner data practices at <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

## Choices and retention

You can clear or block cookies in your browser. You may request access to, edits to, or removal of information you submitted by contacting us.

Submission content may remain visible to preserve the archive. Other information is retained only while needed for the applicable service, legal obligations, or security, then deleted or anonymized where appropriate.
